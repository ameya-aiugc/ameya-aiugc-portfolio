# PROJECT AARVYA

## Project Status
SPEC CREATIVE

## Project Title
One Pair. More Than One Look.

## Project Label
SPEC PROJECT

## Industry
Jewelry / DTC

## Brand

AARVYA
## Product
Chunky gold hoop earrings

## Creative Format

Problem + Solution UGC
## Creative Concept
Your Jewelry Shouldn’t Have an Outfit Limit.

## Target Audience
US women approximately 25–40

## Objective
Demonstrate the versatility of the same pair of chunky gold hoop earrings by showing how they work across an elevated casual daytime look and a dressed-up evening look.

## Hook
Does your jewelry have an outfit limit?

## Creative Approach
A creator-led problem/solution story starts with a relatable jewelry-styling frustration, introduces the AARVYA hoops as the solution, and demonstrates the same product across two contrasting outfits before ending with a clear brand CTA.

## Visual Direction
Premium social-first UGC with natural creator performance, warm-neutral apartment styling, realistic daylight and warm evening lighting, intimate handheld/direct-to-camera moments, product close-ups, mirror/styling shots, and a polished casual-to-evening visual transition.

## Product Storytelling
The same chunky gold hoops remain the central product throughout the creative. The product is introduced through a close product reveal, then worn and demonstrated in both the elevated casual daytime wardrobe and the evening/date-night wardrobe.

## Creator Direction
A fictional US-based female lifestyle/fashion creator, late 20s to early 30s appearance, approachable and confident, with natural performance and conversational delivery. Character identity, facial features, hair, skin tone, and overall appearance remain consistent across the creative.

## Key Creative Mechanism
Same product, different looks — using a casual-to-evening wardrobe transformation to make product versatility visually tangible.

## Why This Ad
The creative turns a common jewelry-styling problem into a simple visual demonstration: the viewer sees the same hoops move from an everyday outfit to a dressed-up look, making the versatility message immediately understandable without relying on unsupported product claims.

## Creative Strengths
- Problem-led UGC hook
- Clear product demonstration through two contrasting looks
- Consistent product and creator continuity
- Social-first storytelling with a clear CTA

## Deliverable
40-second vertical UGC advertisement

## Aspect Ratio
9:16

SPEC CREATIVE — CREATED TO DEMONSTRATE CREATIVE STRATEGY, DIRECTION, AND PRODUCTION CAPABILITY.

## Performance Results

## Creative Breakdown

### Storyboard
A 9-frame storyboard maps the creative from hook and styling problem through product reveal, casual look, wardrobe transition, evening look, payoff, and CTA.

### Character Direction
A consistent fictional female UGC creator is used throughout the advertisement, with locked identity, appearance, wardrobe references, and natural social-first performance.

### Environment Direction
A realistic lived-in apartment bedroom/dressing area provides one consistent environment across the creative, with different camera positions and lighting used for daytime and evening moments.

### Product Direction
A dedicated product reference sheet locks the exact chunky gold hoop design, including geometry, proportions, diameter, thickness, closure, warm gold tone, polished finish, and construction. The same physical product is maintained across all creative shots.

### Final Creative
The finished advertisement presents the same AARVYA hoops across an elevated everyday look and a dressed-up evening look, using creator dialogue, product/lifestyle visuals, captions, on-screen text, music, SFX, and a final brand CTA.

## Project Status

SPEC CREATIVE

## Project Title

Julia Necklace — Storytime / POV UGC Ad

## Project Label

SPEC PROJECT

## Industry

Jewelry / DTC

## Brand

AARVYA
## Product

Julia Necklace — Gold and Red Stone variants, shown individually and
layered

## Creative Format

Storytime / POV UGC
## Creative Concept

Personal style discovery: the creator realizes she does not need a whole
new outfit to make her look more interesting; one good jewelry piece can
change the feel of an outfit.

## Target Audience

Americans / US fashion-lifestyle audience

## Objective

Create a natural-feeling UGC advertisement that presents the Julia
Necklace as a styling discovery and demonstrates how the gold and red
variants can create different looks and work together as a layered
combination.

## Hook

"I finally realized I was overthinking my outfits."

## Creative Approach

The story moves from outfit frustration to product discovery, then from
the gold necklace to the red necklace and finally to the layered
combination. The creator presents the discovery as a personal styling
realization rather than a conventional product pitch.

## Visual Direction

Photorealistic, natural UGC with a warm contemporary American
apartment/dressing-area environment, natural daylight and warm indoor
lighting, realistic human performance, restrained camera movement,
premium commercial quality, and fashion-lifestyle styling.

## Product Storytelling

The product is introduced through three visual states: gold
individually, red individually, and both necklaces layered. Dedicated
product references establish the exact pendant shapes, colors, cords,
proportions, finishes, and layered configuration for continuity.

## Creator Direction

Ava, a 29-year-old White American fashion/lifestyle creator, is directed
to feel warm, observant, confident, slightly witty, and conversational.
Performance is natural and relatable, like casually sharing a style
discovery with a friend rather than delivering a conventional
commercial.

## Key Creative Mechanism

Personal style discovery through progressive product styling: Gold → Red
→ Layered.

## Why This Ad

The creative turns product versatility into a personal story. Instead of
directly claiming that the jewelry can change an outfit, the creator
demonstrates that realization through her own styling progression and
ends with a personal recommendation.

## Creative Strengths

-   Clear story arc from problem to discovery to recommendation
-   Two product variants demonstrated in distinct styling contexts
-   Layering provides a visible creative payoff
-   Product continuity is controlled through dedicated reference assets
-   Natural creator-led UGC communication

## Creative Breakdown

### Storyboard

A locked 9-frame storyboard establishes the hook, outfit problem,
product discovery, gold look, red look, layering reveal, layered detail,
final recommendation, and optional end beauty frame.

### Character Direction

A consistent creator identity is maintained through the CREATOR-01
master character reference, with consistent facial identity, hair,
natural skin texture, body proportions, wardrobe direction, and
conversational UGC performance.

### Environment Direction

The primary setting is a modern American apartment bedroom / dressing
area with a vanity and mirror, warm contemporary styling, natural
daylight as the primary lighting source, and warm indoor lighting as a
secondary look.

### Product Direction

JEW-03A and JEW-03B are treated as authoritative master product
references. The layered configuration uses both necklaces as separate
physical products, preserving their distinct gold and red pendants,
individual black cords, proportions, and construction.

### Final Creative

The finished advertisement demonstrates a storytime/POV UGC approach in
which a creator moves from overthinking her outfits to discovering how
the Julia necklaces can create different styling outcomes. The final
sequence presents the layered combination as the personal-style payoff
and closes with a natural recommendation.

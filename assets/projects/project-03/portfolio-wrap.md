# PROJECT 02

## Project Status

SPEC CREATIVE

## Project Title

Five Rings. More Than Five Looks.

## Project Label

SPEC PROJECT

## Industry

Jewelry / DTC

## Brand

AARVYA
## Product

JEW-02 Stackable Ring Set

## Creative Format

Testimonial + Personal Recommendation + Product Demonstration
## Creative Concept

A creator personally recommends a five-ring set by demonstrating how the
individual rings can be worn alone or combined into different approved
stacks.

## Target Audience

US jewelry / DTC consumers interested in versatile jewelry, personal
style, effortless styling, self-expression, and aesthetic appeal.

## Objective

Product discovery + consideration.

## Hook

“These are five rings... but I swear they give me way more than five
looks."

## Creative Approach

The ad uses a natural creator recommendation structure: introduce the
five-ring collection, show a single ring, build the approved two-ring
stack, transition into the complete five-ring stack, and close with a
personal recommendation and collection CTA.

## Visual Direction

Premium realistic UGC with a natural American creator, warm-neutral
styling, soft natural daylight, believable
apartment/bedroom-dressing-area environments, smartphone-style creator
footage, and controlled product close-ups.

## Product Storytelling

The product is demonstrated through three clear styling states:
individual ring, the approved simple stack of RING-01 + RING-04, and the
approved full five-ring stack of RING-01 + RING-02 + RING-03 + RING-04 +
RING-05. Product continuity is maintained through dedicated individual
and combination references.

## Creator Direction

Emily Carter is used as a warm, confident, relatable American UGC
creator with a natural conversational delivery. Her role is
customer-style testimonial and personal recommendation rather than
polished commercial acting.

## Key Creative Mechanism

Versatility demonstrated through progressive styling: one ring → simple
stack → full stack → personal recommendation.

## Why This Ad

The creative turns the product's versatility into a visible
demonstration rather than relying only on verbal claims. Showing
multiple approved styling configurations helps communicate how one
coordinated set can create different looks.

## Creative Strengths

-   Clear personal recommendation and testimonial structure
-   Strong visual demonstration of product versatility
-   Progressive product reveal from individual ring to full five-ring
    stack
-   Natural UGC presentation combined with premium product storytelling

## Creative Breakdown

### Storyboard

The storyboard covers nine beats: hook with the five-ring collection,
solo ring, adding RING-04, approved simple stack, building the look,
approved full stack, creator reaction, personal recommendation, and
final CTA / collection shot.

### Character Direction

Emily Carter is the locked creator character, with a consistent
appearance, wardrobe, natural American English delivery, and understated
UGC performance across the advertisement.

### Environment Direction

The primary environment is a modern, warm, believable US urban apartment
bedroom/dressing area, with a living area available as the secondary
environment. The visual language uses neutral tones and soft natural
daytime light.

### Product Direction

Dedicated product references establish the individual rings and approved
combinations. REF-R07 is the exact RING-01 + RING-04 simple stack.
REF-R08 is the exact five-ring configuration: RING-01 + RING-02 +
RING-03 + RING-04 + RING-05. Product identity, geometry, proportions,
gemstone placement, metal appearance, and configuration are treated as
continuity locks.

### Final Creative

The finished advertisement demonstrates a creator-led recommendation of
the AARVYA JEW-02 Stackable Ring Set, using product demonstration and
progressive styling to communicate versatility before closing with "I'd
definitely check out the collection."

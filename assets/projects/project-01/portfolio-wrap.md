# PROJECT 01

## Project Status

SPEC CREATIVE

## Project Title

AARVYA — “One Set. Endless Possibilities.”

## Project Label

SPEC PROJECT

## Industry

Luxury Jewelry

## Brand

AARVYA
## Product

Ruby & Diamond Jewelry Set — Necklace, Bracelet, and Ring

## Creative Format

Lifestyle + Voiceover UGC
## Creative Concept

Show how one coordinated jewelry set can move naturally from simple
everyday styling to a more elevated occasion look.

## Target Audience

Modern, sophisticated women interested in elevated jewelry, everyday
luxury, special-occasion styling, and versatile fashion.

## Objective

Product discovery and consideration, with the creative designed to make
the coordinated jewelry set feel desirable, versatile, and relevant to
the viewer's personal style.

## Hook

“If your jewelry only works for one kind of outfit... you need to see
this.”

## Creative Approach

A social-first luxury jewelry advertisement combining the relatability
of UGC with premium jewelry visual language. The creative moves from a
relatable styling problem into product discovery, detailed product
storytelling, versatility, desire, and a collection-focused CTA.

## Visual Direction

Premium cinematic lifestyle imagery with warm natural light, refined
neutral tones, macro jewelry details, controlled camera movement, and a
consistent luxury penthouse environment. The visual language balances
polished jewelry advertising with a natural UGC feel.

## Product Storytelling

The necklace, bracelet, and ring are introduced as a coordinated
collection, with macro details used to highlight the ruby, diamonds,
rose-gold appearance, construction, and light-catching details. The
pieces are then shown worn by the creator in everyday and elevated
lifestyle contexts.

## Creator Direction

Meera is used as the consistent lifestyle / UGC model. Her performance
is confident, graceful, feminine, warm, natural, and understated, with
authentic lifestyle interaction rather than overt commercial posing.

## Key Creative Mechanism

Problem → Product Discovery → Details → Versatility → Desire → CTA

## Why This Ad

The creative frames the jewelry around a relatable styling idea rather
than simply presenting it as a beautiful product. The coordinated set is
demonstrated across lifestyle contexts so the viewer can understand both
its visual appeal and its versatility.

## Creative Strengths

-   Social-first UGC storytelling with premium jewelry execution
-   Clear creative progression from hook to product discovery to CTA
-   Strong product continuity across necklace, bracelet, and ring
-   Macro product storytelling combined with lifestyle integration
-   Consistent creator and environment continuity

35-second 9:16 vertical UGC advertisement

## Creative Breakdown

### Storyboard

A 14-frame visual storyboard covering the hook, product introduction,
jewelry details, everyday styling, everyday-to-evening transition,
desire, and final collection CTA.

### Character Direction

Meera — mid-30s Punjabi Indian female lifestyle / UGC model with long
dark-brown soft waves, warm-medium Indian skin tone, elegant
proportions, and a confident, graceful, approachable presence. Wardrobe
is a fitted floor-length champagne/ivory satin gown.

### Environment Direction

Luxury penthouse interior in Mumbai with modern minimalist styling, warm
neutral tones, large windows, city views, and consistent warm natural
light.

### Product Direction

Dedicated product references were used for the necklace, bracelet, and
ring. The jewelry was treated as distinct physical products while
preserving the established geometry, proportions, gemstone arrangement,
rose-gold appearance, construction, and product details across the
creative.

### Final Creative

The finished advertisement demonstrates premium lifestyle product
storytelling through voiceover, creator-led lifestyle visuals, macro
jewelry details, everyday-to-evening styling, coordinated product
presentation, and a collection-focused CTA.

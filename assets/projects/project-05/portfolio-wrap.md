# PROJECT 03

## Project Status
SPEC CREATIVE

## Project Title
Men's Platinum Wheat Chain — One Piece. Finished Look.

## Project Label
SPEC PROJECT

## Industry
Jewelry / DTC

## Brand

AARVYA
## Product
Men's platinum/silver-white wheat chain necklace

## Creative Format

GRWM + Styling UGC
## Creative Concept
One Piece. Finished Look.

## Target Audience
US men’s jewelry / DTC audience

## Objective
Demonstrate a creator-led jewelry creative that integrates the chain naturally into a GRWM/styling routine while showcasing creative strategy, product storytelling, UGC execution, and performance-creative thinking.

## Hook
“The outfit was fine... it was just missing one thing.”

## Creative Approach
A creator-led GRWM narrative moves from a styling problem to product discovery, wearing and product demonstration, finished-look validation, personal recommendation, and a soft CTA.

## Visual Direction
Modern masculine, premium-casual UGC with cinematic editorial polish, natural creator performance, warm apartment lighting, controlled product close-ups, and restrained social-first styling.

## Product Storytelling
The chain is introduced as the single finishing piece for the outfit, then shown being picked up, worn, examined in macro detail, and integrated into the creator’s finished look. Product continuity is maintained through authoritative product references.

## Creator Direction
A believable American male UGC creator is used as the narrative anchor, with natural, confident, relatable performance rather than high-fashion-model presentation. Primary wardrobe is a black T-shirt.

## Key Creative Mechanism
Problem → Product Discovery → Wear / Demonstrate → Style Payoff → Personal Recommendation → CTA

## Why This Ad
The creative turns a jewelry product into a natural part of an everyday styling decision, using personal recommendation and product demonstration rather than a traditional product-commercial presentation.

## Creative Strengths
- Creator-led UGC storytelling
- Product continuity and reference control
- Natural jewelry styling and demonstration
- Social-first performance-creative structure

## Deliverable
Approximately 30-second vertical UGC advertisement

## Aspect Ratio
9:16

## Spec Work Status
SPEC CREATIVE — CREATED TO DEMONSTRATE CREATIVE STRATEGY, DIRECTION, AND PRODUCTION CAPABILITY.

## Performance Results
Not available — this is a spec project created for portfolio demonstration.

## Creative Breakdown

### Storyboard
An 8-frame storyboard maps the 30-second sequence from hook and styling consideration through product reveal, putting on the chain, product detail, finished look, personal style, and final CTA.

### Character Direction
CHAR-01 establishes the consistent American male creator identity, appearance, wardrobe, natural expressions, and UGC performance direction.

### Environment Direction
ENV-01 establishes a modern American apartment bedroom / dressing area with a mirror, dresser, wardrobe, natural daylight, warm interior lighting, and consistent spatial layout.

### Product Direction
The JEW-03 product reference system establishes one authoritative physical chain, including the master product view, locked master thickness, wheat-chain construction, and attached clasp/end construction.

### Final Creative
The finished advertisement demonstrates a complete GRWM-led jewelry UGC execution combining creator performance, product demonstration, captions, restrained on-screen text, music, SFX, and a creator-led CTA.
